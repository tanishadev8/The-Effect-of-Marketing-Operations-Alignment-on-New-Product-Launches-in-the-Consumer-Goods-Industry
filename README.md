## 📄 Project Overview
Marketing-Operations Alignment (MOA) is a critical factor in the success of **new product launches (NPLs)** in the consumer goods industry. This research explores the **impact of cross-functional coordination** between marketing and operations on launch performance, time-to-market, demand accuracy, customer satisfaction, and business growth.

This repository includes the research report, data findings, methodology, survey analysis, and insights.

---

## 🎯 Research Objectives
- Evaluate the **impact of MOA** on product launch success.
- Study **market responsiveness and supply chain efficiency**.
- Identify **key challenges** in marketing-operations collaboration.
- Analyze the **role of leadership and communication** in alignment.
- Highlight the **importance of technology** and analytics.
- Provide **recommendations** for improving MOA strategies.

## 📊 Key Insights

### 📌 Major Benefits of MOA
- ✅ Faster time-to-market (56.4%)
- ✅ Better demand forecasting (56.4%)
- ✅ Improved customer satisfaction (30.9%)
- ✅ Reduced product launch failures
- ✅ Increased profitability & efficiency

### ⚠️ Major Challenges
- ❗ Lack of communication (50.9%)
- ❗ Conflicting priorities (41.8%)
- ❗ Inaccurate demand forecasting (43.6%)
- ❗ Silo-based working structure
- ❗ Production constraints

### 🛠 Technology in MOA
| Technology Used                            | Adoption |
|--------------------------------------------|-----------|
| ERP Systems                                | 49.1% |
| AI-based demand forecasting                | 49.1% |
| Real-time inventory tracking               | 47.3% |
| Digital supply chain platforms             | 45.5% |
| Project collaboration tools                | 18.2% |

---

## ✅ Recommendations
✔ Establish **cross-functional collaboration frameworks**  
✔ Conduct **regular alignment meetings**  
✔ Use **shared performance metrics (KPIs)**  
✔ Implement **ERP + AI-driven forecasting tools**  
✔ Improve **real-time communication**  
✔ Align strategies under **leadership guidance**  
✔ Promote **data-driven decisions**
